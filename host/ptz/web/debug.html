Axis debug

offline
Raw moves for checking the wiring: a number of microsteps at a speed, without homing, soft limits or travel limits. The axis loses its homing; home it again before normal use. Only the endstop still stops it, when moving toward the homing side. Never plug or unplug a motor while its driver is powered: untick Axis on first.

Driver check (TMC2209)

Open load (coil A / B) is only reliable while the motor turns, at a moderate speed (it is also reported at standstill or in stealthChop). Short to ground or overtemperature: power off and check the wiring.

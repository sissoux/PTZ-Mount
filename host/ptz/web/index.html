Soft limits

Jog the head to a boundary, then press Set. When enabled, every motion (jog, presets, replays, tracking, scripts) stops at the limits.

Base level

Mount standing at an angle: aim the camera level (its electronic level at 0, or the horizon) and press Mark, at two pan angles at least 30° apart. Or type the tilt that looks level at pan 0 and pan +90. Only jogging is corrected; presets and replays go back to exactly where they were taken.

Click a car to follow it: the head goes to the line and waits for that car's next crossing (learning: the lap in progress is dropped; tracking: re-armed). From the timing software: {"cmd":"cars_set","cars":[5,12,44]}, {"cmd":"await_car","car":5} and, at each crossing, {"cmd":"lap","car":5} (UDP port 9000 or HTTP POST /api/cmd). Cars are added to the list as they are seen (12 at most).

1 · Learn the lap

Follow the cars for as many laps as you like and press Space (or LAP) each time a car crosses the start / timing line. On Stop, the laps are averaged into one path at the mean lap time.

2 · Track

ARM moves to the start point and waits. A GO during a lap drops it and glides onto the next one. GO starts the lap: here, with Enter, or from the timing system: UDP {"cmd":"track_go"} to port 9000, or HTTP POST /api/cmd. A joystick move or Stop aborts.
